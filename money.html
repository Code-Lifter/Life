The rulebook

Each rule with where you stand and what a mentor would tell you. Ordered the way most people tackle them.

Rules of thumb for thinking about your money, not personal financial advice. All figures are in today's dollars, using the growth rate you set after inflation. Projections assume your leftover pays off debt first, then gets invested; from your goal age you stop work and live on your current spending until age 100, drawing outside-super money first. Super is generally locked until your preservation age (60 for most people now), so early freedom needs money outside super too. The before-tax income is a rough estimate using 2026–27 Australian resident tax rates, the 2% Medicare levy and the low income tax offset; it ignores HELP debt and other offsets.
