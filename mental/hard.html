HARD Goal Builder

Heartfelt, Animated, Required, Difficult.

Heartfelt (1/4)

What is your ambitious goal, and why do you have a deep, personal emotional connection to it?

Animated (2/4)

Vividly imagine your success. What exactly does your day-to-day life look like once you achieve this?

Required (3/4)

Why is there absolute urgency? Why must this be done right now, instead of next year?

Difficult (4/4)

How does this push you out of your comfort zone? What hard thing are you committing to?
