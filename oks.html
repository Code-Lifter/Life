Objective (1/4)

Key Result 1 (2/4)

What is the primary measurable outcome that proves you are achieving your objective? Include a hard number.

Key Result 2 (3/4) Optional

What is a secondary metric? (Often used as a quality check so you don't compromise standards to hit KR1).

What is your final supporting metric?
